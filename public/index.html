01 — The house

Open to the terrace, quiet at the back.

The Linden House is built as a series of calm rooms around water and glass. The ground floor runs from the living room through the dining room and into a kitchen that meets the terrace.

Upstairs, the primary bedroom looks over the garden. The plan is simple to walk, and the house is set up for a private viewing rather than an open house.
